Privacy Policy

Loop Player · Effective November 14, 2026

Cozyfield Works ("we") respects the privacy of everyone who uses Loop Player (the "app").

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect personal information. There is no sign-up, nothing is sent to our servers, and the app uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

2. Information kept only on your device

InformationPurpose
Settings (subtitle style, display, sound, etc.)Keep your settings the next time you open the app
Server addresses, user names and passwords you add (NAS, PC shared folders, SFTP, FTP, WebDAV)Open videos on your servers — passwords are encrypted in the device Keychain
Resume positions, watch history, Like · Dislike marksResume playback and show your lists
Video and subtitle files you downloadPlayback

None of this is sent to anyone, including us, and it is removed when you delete the app.

3. Permissions

PermissionUsed for
PhotosList and play videos from your Photos library (nothing leaves your device)
Local networkFind and connect to NAS and PC shared folders on your Wi-Fi, and cast to a TV

4. Network connections

The app connects only to servers and TVs (Chromecast, AirPlay) that you add or choose. These connections are directly between your device and that server or TV; we cannot see them.

5. Sharing and transfers

Because we collect no personal information, nothing is shared with third parties, outsourced, or transferred abroad.

6. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with its effective date.
